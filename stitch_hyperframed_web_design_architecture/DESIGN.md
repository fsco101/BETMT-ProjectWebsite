## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the quiet confidence of haute ébénisterie, high-end architectural millwork, and industrial artisanal craft. It is crafted for discerning clients, architects, interior directors, and patrons of custom luxury craftsmanship.

The visual mood evokes an architectural studio and a clean-lined modern workshop: natural light across raw timber, calibrated stone surfaces, and deliberate precision. The design style relies on **high-contrast editorial minimalism** fused with delicate architectural structures. It balances museum-like negative space with razor-thin structural hair lines, Romanesque arched apertures, and dignified typographic scale. The interface steps back, allowing materiality, process photography, and artisanal portraits to command center stage with calm authority and timeless permanence.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the natural materials of an artisan wood atelier: pale lime wash, dry stone, aged oak, and deep mineral carbon. 

- **Canvas & Backgrounds:** The primary background uses `canvas-stone` (`#FAF9F5`) and warm `surface-linen` (`#EAE7E1`), establishing an tactile, paper-like warmth that softens contrast while maintaining crisp visibility.
- **Ink & Typography:** Primary typographic marks and structural anchors are set in `charcoal-ink` (`#1C1B1A`) rather than harsh pure black, preserving an organic, print-press feel. Secondary annotations and body prose utilize `secondary_color_hex` (`#736C65`).
- **Dividers & Structural Rules:** Structural hairline dividers are rendered in `hairline-divider` (`#D6D1C7`), providing whisper-light architectural scaffolding without visual friction.
- **Accents:** Warm wood tones are referenced via `wood-terracotta` (`#C8956A`), reserved strictly for subtle focus states, micro-badges, or bespoke interactive indicators.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy delivers an authoritative editorial rhythm.

- **Display & Headlines:** Headings use `Bodoni Moda` in uppercase or classic sentence case. It delivers the ultra-refined, high-contrast, razor-thin serifs reminiscent of bespoke architectural monographs. Headlines must be tracked out slightly with wide line heights to breathe.
- **Body & Secondary Copy:** `Work Sans` provides an unobtrusive, modern structural counterweight. Kept at a clean regular weight (`400`) and standard size, it preserves maximum legibility across editorial descriptions.
- **Labels & Micro-Copy:** Metadata, craft titles, status flags, and timestamps use `label-uppercase` set in all caps with generous tracking (`0.14em`), anchoring portraits and gallery elements with architectural precision.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a 12-column architectural fluid grid governed by generous outer margins and expansive negative space between narrative sections.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with `2.5rem` gutters and outer margins of `4.5rem`. Editorial narrative sections utilize an asymmetric structure: large titling offset on columns 1–7, with contextual paragraphs and dates aligned on columns 8–12.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and `2.5rem` margins. Craftsperson portrait grids reflow smoothly from 3 columns to 2 columns.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.5rem` margins. Sections stack vertically with generous vertical spacing (`space-xl`) between milestones to maintain dignity without density crowding.
- **Architectural Dividers:** Thin hairline rules (1px `#D6D1C7`) separate major editorial movements. Vertical alignment marks and perpendicular hairpins intersect section boundaries to emphasize precision framing.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **flat tonal layering and architectural hairpins**, avoiding skeuomorphic dropped shadows.

- **Surface Tiers:** Depth is articulated strictly through delicate shifts in background tone—transitioning from pure `canvas-stone` (`#FAF9F5`) to inset `surface-linen` (`#EAE7E1`) containers.
- **Hairline Framing:** Outlines and dividers are rendered with crisp 1px borders in `hairline-divider` (`#D6D1C7`). Overlapping architectural cards use thin borders rather than drop shadows.
- **Architectural Arches:** Recessed apertures, historical archive frames, and contact backdrops employ subtle planar depth: an arch-shaped mask over images or a softly tinted linen backdrop silhouette placed behind text.
- **Interactive Depth:** Hovering elements (such as portrait cards or navigation links) triggers subtle opacity shifts, underline reveals, or gentle micro-scaling (1.01x) without introducing elevated shadows.

## Shapes

The primary shape philosophy is **Sharp (0px)**, reflecting the measured cuts, squared timber, and exact joinery of custom architectural woodworking.

- **Frames & Cards:** All standard cards, container boxes, buttons, and input boundaries have crisp 90-degree right angles (`0px` border-radius).
- **Architectural Arch Signature:** As a curated counterpoint to the sharp grid, specific hero imagery, historical milestones (e.g., the 1991 workshop arch), and focal containers utilize a traditional semicircular arch shape (`border-radius: 9999px 9999px 0 0` on top edges). This arch acts as a hallmark motif, contrasting the otherwise sharp, rectilinear format.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Links
- **Primary Button:** Crisp rectangular form, solid `charcoal-ink` (`#1C1B1A`) background, off-white text, uppercase typography (`label-uppercase`), generous horizontal padding (`space-lg`), no rounded corners. Hover state transitions to soft subtle opacity or `wood-terracotta` border highlight.
- **Ghost / Text Action:** Pure typography underlined with a delicate 1px hairline set 4px beneath the baseline. Hover state subtly shifts ink color to `secondary_color_hex`.
- **Media Controls:** Circular or cleanly squared icon triggers with thin 1px outlines, housing fine arrow glyphs or play wedges.

### Portrait & Craftsperson Cards
- **Grid Layout:** 3-column desktop arrangement with crisp vertical dividing lines or uniform gutters.
- **Imagery:** Black-and-white or desaturated workshop portrait photography cropped to uniform 4:5 aspect ratios. Zero corner radius.
- **Captions:** Positioned directly below the image with zero excess wrapper styling. Name set in `title-lg` or `body-md` in medium weight, followed by trade/role (e.g., "Ébéniste", "Président", "Opérateur CNC") set in `label-uppercase` in `#736C65`.

### Editorial Workshop Arches & Milestones
- **Archive Display:** Historical vignettes set inside a semicircular arch frame, anchored by high-contrast serif numeral dates (e.g., "1991" in `Bodoni Moda`) overlapping the base of the image.
- **Carousel Anchors:** Flanked by delicate chevron navigation arrows with ample breathing room.

### Form Inputs & Newsletter Subscription
- **Text Inputs:** Minimalist underline-only or single 1px hairline perimeter fields over `#FAF9F5`. No ambient shadows. Placeholder text styled in quiet, muted neutral `#736C65`.
- **Submission Trigger:** Monogram or minimalist right-arrow glyph nestled cleanly flush right inside the input field.

### Dividers & Section Markers
- **Hairline Rules:** 1px horizontal lines spanning full column width or framed with vertical end-caps, evoking architectural draft blueprints.
- **Brand Stamp:** Square black monolith monogram badge (`M` / `LM`) embedded with strict architectural symmetry within footer cards and navigation anchor points.
