THE 8-STAGE AIR DISTRIBUTION CIRCUIT

Main Ring Loop · ISO 8573 Purity Standards
Stage 01
01. Central Compressor Primary Atmospheric Induction

Industrial dual-stage reciprocating compressor drawing ambient air, pressurizing it to baseline workshop pressure inside a vibration-isolated and acoustically dampened enclosure.

Capacity / Motor 15 kW / 20 HP
Stage 02
02. Particulate Filter 1 Mechanical Coarse Separation

Cyclonic and sintered bronze filter element capturing gross solid debris, rust scale, and heavy liquid water droplets before raw air enters the refrigeration cycle.

Threshold 5.0 Microns
Stage 03
03. Refrigerant Air Dryer Vapor Condensation Loop

Thermodynamically cools the compressed airstream to force moisture vapor to condense and exhaust through timed automatic solenoids, preventing corrosion in distribution pipes.

Pressure Dew Point +3°C (37°F)
Stage 04
04. Coalescing Filter 2 Sub-Micron Aerosol Scrub

High-efficiency borosilicate microfiber matrix capturing residual sub-micron lubricating oil mists and fine particulates, purifying the stream to Class 1/2 standards.

Purity Class 0.01 Micron

Vertical ASME pressure vessel equipped with calibrated relief valve, absorbing pneumatic pulse surges, dampening compressor cycles, and storing buffer capacity.

ASME Volume 500 L / 132 Gal

Overhead powder-coated ISO blue ring manifold pitched at 1:100 slope to ensure balanced flow velocity without dead-ends or stagnant moisture pockets.

Standard Operating Protocol 1.1
High-Velocity Particle & Acoustic Containment

Operating at 6.0–7.0 Bar generates high-velocity airborne swarf, carbide debris, and localized noise levels exceeding 92 dBA. Polycarbonate eyewear prevents corneal laceration, while dual-earcup acoustic dampers mitigate hearing injury.

Standard: ANSI Z87.1 Acoustic Guard: NRR 28dB Status: Active
Tethered Whip-Check Inspection
Standard Operating Protocol 1.2
Mechanical Hose Fatigue & Coupler Locking

Tears or microscopic fatigue pinholes under 100 PSI pressure can propagate into catastrophic burst fractures within milliseconds. The galvanized steel whip-check prevents whip lash injury if a coupling separates.

Burst Rating: 20 Bar Cable: Galv Steel
≥ 100mm Standoff Clearance
Standard Operating Protocol 1.3
Deflection Physics & Chip Recoil Prevention

Holding nozzles closer than 100 mm creates backpressure that ricochets razor-sharp metal turnings straight back toward the operator's face and arms. Shield discs direct airflow downward.

Standoff: ≥ 100 mm Deflector: Polycarbonate
Zero Residual PSI Exhaust
Standard Operating Protocol 1.4
Stored Kinetic Discharge & 5S Coiling

Disconnecting pressurized couplings releases explosive backflow capable of propelling brass plugs like ballistic projectiles. Depressurizing to absolute 0 PSI prior to collar retraction completely neutralizes mechanical hazard.

OSHA 1910.242(b) Nozzle Residual: 0 PSI

STRICT PROHIBITIONS (DON'T)

Zero Tolerance · Critical Safety Violations
Lethal Embolism Hazard
Critical Failure Mode 01
Arterial Gas Penetration & Acoustic Blast

Even at pressures as low as 15 PSI, air can penetrate the epidermis through microscopic skin pores, entering the vascular bloodstream. The resulting air bubbles travel directly to heart chambers and cerebral arteries, inducing fatal cardiac arrest.

Violation: Fatal Hazard Lethal Entry: > 10 PSI
Tamper-Sealed ASME Valve
Critical Failure Mode 02
Pressure Vessel Rupture & Shrapnel Overpressure

The ASME safety relief valve on the 500-liter buffer tank is calibrated to trigger at 9.5 Bar. Defeating this valve risks explosive vessel fragmentation capable of collapsing workshop perimeter walls.

Set Point: 9.5 Bar Max Standard: ASME Sec VIII
Hose Fatigue Hazard
Critical Failure Mode 03
Polymer Shearing & Violent Hose Whipping

Crimping concentrates pneumatic hydraulic shock at an acute fold point. When the inner braid shears, the hose separates violently, whipping wildly across machine tables at supersonic speeds.

Banned: Hose Kinking Correct: 1/4 Turn Lever
Max 90 PSI Tool Rating
Critical Failure Mode 04
Internal Turbine Over-speed & Shrapnel Ejection

Pneumatic die grinders and turbines are engineered for a maximum 90 PSI. Connecting them to unregulated 120+ PSI lines causes bearing runaway speeds, shattering abrasive discs into high-speed shrapnel.

Max Limit: 90 PSI (6.2 Bar) PVC Air Piping: BANNED

THE 7-STEP OPERATING PROCEDURE

Ref: SOP-PNEU-REV-04 · Step-by-Step Illustrated Protocol
Step 01 Verification
01

Pre-Operational Visual Inspection

Examine the wall-mounted drop receiver manifold, column pressure gauge, fittings, and air drain bowl. Confirm absolute absence of audible hissing, physical cracks, or loose mounting hardware before connecting any hose.

Zero Leakage Visual & Acoustic Baseline Pass
Step 02 Verification
02

Hose Connection & Whip-Check Engagement

Slide back the quick-connect female sleeve and insert the hardened brass tool plug until hearing an audible snap click. Fasten the steel braided whip-check cable firmly across both joint ends to arrest accidental decouple whip.

Coupling: Positive Snap Tethered Whip Guard Engaged
Step 03 Verification
03

Operating Pressure Verification

Read the localized analog drop dial gauge. Confirm indicator needle stabilizes strictly inside the calibrated safe zone between 6.0 and 7.0 Bar (87 to 100 PSI). Adjust regulator knob clockwise or counter-clockwise if out of range.

Tolerance: ±0.2 Bar Calibrated 6.0 - 7.0 Bar
Step 04 Verification
04

Smooth Ball Valve Opening

Grasp the yellow quarter-turn isolation handle and sweep smoothly 90° into parallel alignment with the downward pipe. Smooth opening prevents hydraulic pressure shock waves from violently stressing the hose core.

Rotation: 90° Sweep No Hydraulic Pressure Hammer
Step 05 Verification
05

Chip Clearing & Tool Operation

Maintain minimum 100 mm (4 in) standoff distance from the workpiece surface. Direct high-speed metal swarf at 45° strictly into machine chip trays and catch guards; never toward personnel aisles.

Distance: ≥ 100 mm Chip Deflector Guard Active
Step 06 Verification
06

Valve Isolation & Pressure Depletion

Rotate the wall isolation lever 90° perpendicular (shut position). Point blowgun into machine chip enclosure and press trigger until air hissing entirely ceases, venting internal line pressure to exact 0 PSI.

Exhaust: 0 PSI Stored Kinetic Energy Neutralized
Step 07 Verification
07

Disconnection & 5S Coil Storage

With 0 PSI confirmed, retract the coupler sleeve smoothly to release the plug. Neatly wind the pneumatic hose in uniform 300 mm loops and hang upon the dedicated wall saddle bracket, keeping shop walkways clean.

Standard: 5S Workshop 300mm Neat Saddle Hanging
